Reading path, for the family tree to make sense

Every series is billed as a standalone, but the "who's related to whom" payoff builds in publication order.

  1. Kings of Kroydon HillsFounding cast — Ryans, Murphys, Sinclairs, and the first Kingston/Beneventi match.
  2. Restless KingsNext generation of the same circle.
  3. Risks We TakeSide duology, same world — optional but quick.
  4. Defiant KingsYounger Kingston half-siblings.
  5. Playing to WinThe families start marrying into one another — read this before Red Lips & White Lies.
  6. Red Lips & White LiesThe big interconnected one — the kids grow up.
  7. Love & LegacyCurrently releasing — the grandkids and cousins.

The family tree

One tree, not six — every couple nested under their actual parents. Where someone marries into a line shown elsewhere in the tree, they're noted rather than repeated.

The Sinclairs, Beneventis, and Murphys don't share a documented common ancestor, so the tree runs as four trunks that intermarry — read the notes to follow a person across trunks.
